Use case: stylized-concept.
Create a single square 4 by 4 fantasy dungeon TILE sheet, exactly 16 different simple square terrain tiles in a precise regular 4x4 grid. This is a tile atlas for a 64-by-64 pixel grid-based roguelike. Source image approximately 1280 by 1280 pixels; the entire sheet is roughly 256 logical pixels across before upscaling. Large approximately five-pixel square pseudo-pixels, coherent chunky pixel clusters, hard staircase edges, no tiny high-resolution detail.

Style: simple pixel art inspired by early DOS fantasy strategy games and the first Heroes of Might and Magic, with a gentle naive-painting feeling. Same chunky, readable two- or three-tone shading as a sheet of small fantasy heroes and monsters. Muted middle-value dungeon surfaces support brightly colored characters. Purple-charcoal crevices, weathered grey-brown stones with cool grey highlights, restrained moss greens, dark teal blue water, warm brown doors. No photorealism, no smooth rendering, no gradients, no noisy dithering.

Composition: the 16 equal square tiles cover the ENTIRE image edge to edge. Four equal rows and four equal columns, zero gutters, zero padding, zero surrounding background, no decorative tile borders or grid lines. Every cell is a completely filled opaque square. Each tile is a simple independent terrain image; no autotile sets, corners, end caps, transition pieces, shorelines or diagonal shapes. A straight overhead view for floor and water; traditional compact front-facing masonry and doorway glyphs for walls and doors, compatible with a top-down old fantasy game. Large simple readable forms, consistent detail density throughout.

Tiles in row-major order:
Row 1, four walkable stone floors: broad grey stone slabs; slightly cracked grey stone slabs; grey stone slabs with a little moss between stones; worn irregular stone paving. Keep them subdued and flat, with few broad joints, no objects.
Row 2, four solid wall cells: rough dark grey stone masonry; larger square ancient stone blocks; slightly mossy dark stone masonry; weathered grey-brown dungeon bricks. Masonry fills each square completely, walls clearly darker and heavier than floor cells. No corner or terminal wall pieces.
Row 3, four full water cells: deep dark teal water with sparse simple blue ripples; still blue water with broad reflected streaks; greenish underground water with sparse muted ripples; dark blue water with a few broad wave clusters. Water fills every cell completely; no land, islands, shore or banks.
Row 4, four closed doors embedded in a full-cell grey stone wall: plain vertical brown wooden planks; reinforced wooden door with two dark horizontal iron straps; old wooden door with a simple small brass lock; dark arched wooden dungeon door with an iron lattice. Door designs occupy most of their cell with integrated stone masonry, no empty margin.

Constraints: no creatures, people, heroes, props, skull piles, torches, text, labels, letters, numbers, logos or watermark. No cutout sprites and no transparency. Do not draw a square outline around every tile. The sheet must be exactly a 4x4 grid of full-bleed terrain squares. Each floor and each water variant should repeat as a simple texture, with similar color values along opposite edges.
