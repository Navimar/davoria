Use case: stylized-concept.
Asset type: ONE collectible strength-crystal sprite for a Godot dungeon game using a 64x64 logical pixel grid, delivered as a standalone transparent PNG.
Primary request: a small, clearly readable cyan-blue magical crystal pickup. One tall faceted prism with two short facets at its base, a compact single crystal silhouette. Six to eight flat colors: deep navy-purple outline, blue shadows, turquoise/cyan midtones, pale icy highlights. Match restrained chunky classic DOS fantasy pixel art and an AAP64-like palette.
Composition: square transparent 64x64 logical-pixel canvas. The crystal itself is deliberately small, about 24 logical pixels wide and 30-34 logical pixels high, centered in the canvas, with transparent space around it. Draw coarse facets with only a few large planes and a one-logical-pixel dark outline; avoid detailed illustration that becomes noisy after pixelization. No objects outside the crystal. A single small bright highlight on a facet is enough.
Constraints: actual transparent background, hard pixel edges, no blurry glow, no bloom, no floor, no pedestal, no cast shadow, no ring, no stars or particles, no typography, no border, no checkerboard painted into the image. The item should remain distinct on brown stone floors with green moss and gray walls. The sprite is a foreground collectible and must not be dimmed like terrain. No animation sheet; exactly one crystal pickup, with simple front/top three-quarter facets.
